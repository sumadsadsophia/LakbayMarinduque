Welcome to Marinduque

A heart-shaped island with more than one festival to its name.

Marinduque is home to the Moriones Holy Week tradition, and also to white-sand beaches, island sandbars, caves, waterfalls, Mt. Malindig and heritage churches. Browse the places below, then build your own itinerary.
